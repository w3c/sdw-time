:disjoint
IRI:
http://www.w3.org/2006/time#disjoint
Definition:
If T1 disjoint T2, then the beginning of T1 is after the end of T2, or the end of T1 is before the beginning of T2. The two entities do not overlap or coincide in any way, but their ordering relationship is not known.
