Alignments

Alignment with BFO/CCO

This section introduces the alignment of OWL-Time with the Basic Formal Ontology ([[BFO]]) and Common Core Ontologies ([[CCO]]).

The ontology IRI of the BFO/CCO Alignment module is http://www.w3.org/2006/time/bfo
The version IRI of the BFO/CCO Alignment module 2023 edition is http://www.w3.org/2006/time/2026/bfo

Namespaces

The following namespace prefixes are used in the alignment of SSN with BFO and CCO.

: (default)
http://www.w3.org/2006/time#
obo:
http://purl.obolibrary.org/obo/
cco:
https://www.commoncoreontologies.org/
Class Alignments

The primary classes from OWL-Time are aligned with the BFO classes as follows.

The definition of each BFO concept is displayed as a tooltip when hovering over the concept.

  • obo:BFO_0000203
    "temporal instant"     ≡ :Instant
  • obo:BFO_0000202
    "temporal interval"     ≡ :ProperInterval
Property Alignments

The definition of each BFO or CCO concept is displayed as a tooltip when hovering over the concept.
